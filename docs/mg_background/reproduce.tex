\section{Implementation and reproduction}
\label{sec:impl}\label{sec:reproduce}
\begingroup\sloppy

\paragraph{Implementation.} The method is implemented in RutticeQCD (Rust), on
the CPU and on NVIDIA GPUs through CUDA kernels of the Wilson and M\"obius
operators taken from Bridge++. On the device the fine operators, the
prolongation, the coarse operators and the coarse Krylov solvers all stay on the
GPU. The precisions are those of Table~\ref{tab:setups}: the prolongation is stored
in half precision unless asked otherwise, the cycle above the coarse lattice runs in
single precision on operators converted on the device from the links of the
double-precision ones, and the outer solver is in double precision. The coarse
solvers are BiCGStab for the quark factor and MINRES on $\Hc$ for the Pauli--Villars
factor (Sec.~\ref{sec:coarse}), both stopped at $|r|^2/|b|^2<10^{-2}$ unless stated,
on the even coarse sites. The numbers of Sec.~\ref{sec:results} come from the
parameter-file program \texttt{ruttice} and from the examples
\texttt{preconditioned\_fov} (CPU) and \texttt{preconditioned\_fov\_device} (GPU),
whose options are listed in Table~\ref{tab:provenance}; the checks of
Eq.~\eqref{eq:exact} (Sec.~\ref{sec:basis}) from the example \texttt{correction\_exactness} (CPU). The
numbers of Sec.~\ref{sec:dagger} come from the examples \texttt{mobius\_gamma5\_check}
(Eqs.~\eqref{eq:g5herm} and \eqref{eq:g5exact}, CPU), \texttt{dminus\_cost} (the inverse of
$D_-$, GPU), \texttt{dual\_basis} (prolongations for the dual cycle, GPU) and
\texttt{dagger\_precond} (Table~\ref{tab:c4}, GPU). In HMC the solves with $D\dg$ use
$\Gfive\mathcal M\Gfive$ (\texttt{fermion::adjoint\_by\_gamma5}).
The eigensolvers (thick-restart Lanczos, Krylov--Schur Arnoldi~\cite{Stewart} with
left eigenvectors, and $\sigma_{\min}(z-X)$ by Lanczos on $(z-X)\dg(z-X)$) are in the
module \texttt{eigen} and are checked against Bridge++'s eigensolver test.

All numbers of Sec.~\ref{sec:results} come from one configuration per volume; the
most useful extensions are more configurations, a larger volume and dynamical
ensembles. The rest of this appendix gives what is needed to run them.

\paragraph{Code.} RutticeQCD, \url{https://github.com/atsanroger/RutticeQCD}, branch
\texttt{cuda-bridge} (the commit of each result is in Table~\ref{tab:provenance}; later commits
give the same iterations, up to the differences of a few iterations between builds noted in
Sec.~\ref{sec:setups}). It needs Rust (stable) and, for the GPU, CUDA~12:
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
  export CUDA_HOME=/usr/local/cuda
  cargo build --release --features cuda-bridge
  ./target/release/ruttice mg_24.yaml
\end{verbatim}}
The GPU backend runs on one GPU. The parameter files are in the style of Bridge++
and are documented in \texttt{docs/USAGE.md}; gauge configurations are read in the
Bridge++ text and binary formats, NERSC, ILDG and SciDAC.

Builds add the device reductions in different orders. Against the earlier build \texttt{6c7ff42}
the multigrid iterations of Table~\ref{tab:massscan} differ by one or two in six of the 28 runs,
and those of Table~\ref{tab:costs} differed by up to six with an earlier build; whether a code
change or the order of the reductions causes the first is not established.

\paragraph{The mass scan (Table~\ref{tab:massscan}, Fig.~\ref{fig:massscan}).} The
parameter files are \texttt{docs/mg\_background/runs/mg\_24.yaml} (the multigrid) and
\texttt{runs/cgne\_24.yaml} (the reference, double or mixed precision); the comments
in them say how to switch to setup~B and to a point source. Each run prints a line
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
  seed 91: GCR 28 iterations on the device; 3014 coarse applications;
      worst |Dx-b|^2 2e-18; 13.2 s; ... device 19.91 GB
\end{verbatim}}
and, for the multigrid, \texttt{setup on the device: 206 s}. These, per mass and
setup, are what Table~\ref{tab:massscan} lists. On one GPU one configuration takes about
$50$ minutes for the multigrid (seven masses, both setups, $\alpha=0.6$; the setup
dominates) and under half an hour for the two CGNE scans.

\paragraph{Memory and volume.} On $24^3\times48$ the multigrid needs $19.9$\,GB of
device memory (CGNE $6.9$\,GB in double, $7.2$\,GB mixed), roughly proportional to the
volume: $32^3\times64$ needs about $63$\,GB (an $80$\,GB GPU), $48^3\times96$ more
than one GPU has. The aggregates, $4^4$, must divide the lattice, and each level
below the coarse lattice must divide the extent of the level above
(\texttt{MultiGrid\_Level2}, \texttt{\_Level3}; on $32^3\times64$, for instance,
super-blocks $2$ and $2$).

\paragraph{The spectral quantities (Secs.~\ref{sec:fov}--\ref{sec:pseudo}).} The
example \texttt{preconditioned\_fov\_device} computes, with exact coarse solves, the
field of values, the parts of the cycle, the eigenvalues with their condition
numbers, and $\sigma_{\min}(z-\tilde A\mathcal M)$ on contours, e.g.
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
  cargo run --release --features cuda-bridge \
      --example preconditioned_fov_device -- \
      16 16 binary conf.bin 1.0 0.001 1 --ratio 10 \
      --arnoldi --far 1,0 --pseudo --contour 1,0,0.6,16
\end{verbatim}}
(arguments: $L$, $T$, format, file, $M_5$, $m$, stout $0/1$). It fits $16^4$ in $24$\,GB
and takes about an hour there; $24^3\times48$ does not fit. The CPU examples
\texttt{preconditioned\_fov}, \texttt{coarse\_hermiticity} and
\texttt{correction\_exactness} need no GPU; the last, e.g.
{\small
\begin{verbatim}
  cargo run --release --example correction_exactness -- \
      12 binary conf.bin 1.0 0.001 1 --block 4
\end{verbatim}}
\noindent prints $\|e(r)-y\|/\|y\|$ for the right form, the left form and the dual
correction, and the GCR(2) iterations of $\AR$ and $\AR\dg$ (the smoother of setup~S
by default, \texttt{--steps 12 --ratio 30} for setup~B; the exactness checks take about
$12$ minutes on $12^4$ on a desktop CPU).

\paragraph{What would help most.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The mass scan of Table~\ref{tab:massscan} on three to five further
$24^3\times48$ configurations, for the spread of the iterations and of the
speed-up.
\item The same on a larger volume ($32^3\times64$ on an $80$\,GB GPU), for the volume
dependence of the multigrid iterations and of the setup.
\item The mass scan on a dynamical ($2+1$-flavor) ensemble, e.g.\ that of
Ref.~\cite{KCM2024}, where the spectra of Sec.~\ref{sec:spectra} were taken.
\item The spectral run above on further $16^4$ configurations, for how the outliers
of setup~S and the pseudospectra vary between configurations.
\end{enumerate}
For each run, the result lines above and the commit of the build are enough; the
logs contain everything else.
